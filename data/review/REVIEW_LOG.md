# Test split: label review log

The test split (`data/test.jsonl`) was labelled and reviewed **before any verifier configuration was run on it**,
then frozen (`data/MANIFEST.json`). No prompt or pipeline change was made after that point.

## Who did what

| Step | Annotator | What it saw | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Draft | Claude (the author's coding assistant) | guideline, passages, answers | 269 claims |
| 2. Independent-model audit | qwen3.8-max, kimi-k3 (minimal prompt, oracle mode; **not** the base model, not the optimized pipeline) | passages, claim texts | agreement with draft 0.955 / 0.929; 12 disagreements adjudicated by the guideline; 4 labels changed |
| 3. Second review | a separate Claude agent | guideline + full review file (gold included) | 7 clear and 14 debatable issues; 19 accepted (5 missing claims added, evidence sets completed, contestable flags made consistent, 6 labels changed); 1 not applied (splitting fact+reason claims; no label would change) |
| 4. Blind agreement | (a) a Claude Sonnet agent; (b) deepseek-v4.1-flash via `data/annotate.py` | guideline, passages, answer, claim text; **no gold** | see below |

All changes are recorded in the affected claim's `note` field.

## Blind agreement (step 4)

A stratified sample of 40 claims (12 C, 10 U, 18 S; seed 7) was drawn from the **non-contestable** claims after
step 2. It was kept fixed through step 3 so the finished labels stay comparable. One sampled claim
(`test-sqlite-limits-natural c3`) was marked contestable during step 3. Its label did not change.

| Second annotator | Raw agreement | Cohen's kappa |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Sonnet agent | 40 / 40 | 1.00 |
| deepseek-v4.1-flash | 40 / 40 | 1.00 |

### Caveats

- **The second annotators are LLMs, not people.** No human re-labelling has been done yet; the files under
  `data/review/` support it (`python -m data.review agreement`).
- The sample excludes contestable claims by design. Perfect agreement therefore shows that the non-contestable labels
  are unambiguous under the guideline. It says nothing about the 22 contestable claims, which headline metrics exclude.
- deepseek-v4.1-flash is also the verifier's base model. Its labels were used only to measure agreement, never to
  change gold.
